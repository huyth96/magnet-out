# Privacy Policy: Magnet Out

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Magnet Out ("the game") is a single-player puzzle game for Android, made by Moss Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game and the services inside it use, why, who receives it,
and the choices you have. It applies to the game as distributed on Google Play.

## Summary

- There are no accounts and no sign-in. We run no servers for the game, and we do not receive your game data.
- Your progress and settings are saved on your device.
- The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising ID and other data described in section 2.
  Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in the
  game: Settings > Privacy options.
- Optional purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
- Gameplay statistics: see section 4.

## 1. Data saved on your device

The game saves the following in its private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read:

- **Progress:** stars and best tap counts per level, unlocked levels, coins, boosters, Daily Challenge results, daily
  rewards and streaks, missions and achievements.
- **Settings:** sound, music, vibration, language and graphics quality.
- **Purchase records:** which items you own (for example Remove ads), and a one-way fingerprint (an MD5 hash) of each
  purchase token, so that one purchase is never delivered twice.
- **Ad timing:** when the last ads were shown and how many were shown today, so that the game can space them out.
- **Play statistics:** numbers such as wins, attempts, sessions and total play time, used by the game itself (for
  example to decide when an ad or a tip may appear).

We do not receive any of this. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.
If backup is turned on for your device, Android may copy the save to the backup of your Google account and restore
it on a new phone. Google provides that backup under its own privacy policy; we cannot access it.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free to play and is funded by ads. It shows:

- **rewarded ads,** only when you choose to watch one, for example to get extra taps, a booster or coins;
- **full-screen (interstitial) ads** at natural breaks: as a level ends (won or failed), before its result
  screen, never in the middle of a level.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a Google service, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game. To show ads,
measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, and detect and prevent fraud, the SDK may collect the following
and share it with Google:

- your device's **advertising ID** and **app set ID**;
- your **IP address**, which is used to estimate your general location (such as your country or city);
- **interactions** with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
- **diagnostic data**, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use;
- basic **device information**, such as the device model, Android version and language.

This data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Depending on your choices and where you live, the ads you see may be
**personalised** (based on your activity in other apps and websites) or **non-personalised** (based on context, such
as this game and your general location).

**Your choices**

- **Consent.** In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form (the Google User
  Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can accept, refuse, or choose purpose by purpose. You can play
  the whole game whatever you choose; if you refuse, Google may still show limited or non-personalised ads.
- **Change or withdraw your choice** at any time: open the game, then Settings > Privacy options.
- **Advertising ID.** You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your device
  settings (on most phones: Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads; the path depends on the phone).
- **Ad settings at Google:** https://myadcenter.google.com

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The game offers optional in-app purchases, such as coin packs and Remove ads, through the Google Play billing system.
Google Play processes the payment. We never receive your card number, bank details or billing address. Google Play
tells the game which item was bought, with its purchase status and a purchase token, so that the game can deliver
the item; the game keeps only a fingerprint of the token on your device (section 1). Google may also show us basic
order details in our Google Play Console, such as the order number, the item, the price, the country and the date,
which we use only for refunds, support and accounting.

Google Play Terms of Service: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Gameplay statistics (analytics)

The game contains no analytics service and no crash-reporting service. It does not send gameplay statistics or
crash reports to us or to any analytics provider; the play statistics of section 1 stay on your device. Ad
measurement by AdMob is described in section 2.

If your phone shares usage and diagnostics data with Google (an Android setting), Google may use crash and
performance data from the game to show us aggregated quality reports in the Google Play Console, such as crash
counts by device model and Android version. These reports do not tell us who you are.

## 5. Other features

- **This policy in the game.** Settings > Privacy Policy shows this policy inside the game; no internet connection
  is needed to read it.
- **Share and "Ask a friend".** These buttons make a picture of the puzzle board (with your stars and taps on it) and
  a short text with a link to the game's Google Play page, then open Android's share menu. Nothing is shared until you
  pick an app and send it yourself, and nothing is sent to us. The link carries only a label saying it came from a
  share (for example "utm_source=share"), which Google Play may count in our store statistics without telling us who
  you are. The picture is kept in the game's own storage on your device until it is replaced by the next share.
- **Rate on Google Play.** Settings > Rate on Google Play opens the game's Google Play page. Ratings and reviews are
  handled by Google Play under its terms; we see only what Google Play shows publicly.
- **Email us / Send feedback.** Settings > Send feedback opens your own mail app with our support address and the game
  version, your phone's operating system name and the game language filled in; you decide whether to send it. If you
  write to us (this way or from our Google Play page), we receive your email address and what you write. We use it
  only to answer you and to improve the game, and we delete it 12 months after the conversation ends.
- **Vibration.** Used for haptic feedback only; no data is collected.

## 6. Permissions

The game asks Android for: internet and network state access (for ads and purchases), the advertising ID permissions
(`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` and `android.permission.AD_ID`, used by AdMob), Google Play billing
(`com.android.vending.BILLING`), and vibration. Google's ads SDK also declares Android's ad services permissions
(`android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID`, `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION` and `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS`), which
Android's privacy-preserving ad features use, and the wake lock and foreground service permissions
(`android.permission.WAKE_LOCK`, `android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE`), which its background task scheduler
(Android WorkManager) uses to finish short ad-related tasks. Android's support library adds one permission that only
the game itself can hold (`com.mossstudio.magnetout.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`). The game does not ask
for your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or phone.

## 7. Who receives data

We do not sell your personal data for money. Data is received only by:

- **Google** (AdMob and Google Play), to provide ads and purchases, as described above;
- **authorities,** if the law requires it.

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards:
https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks

## 8. How long data is kept

- **On your device:** until you uninstall the game or clear its storage.
- **At Google:** under Google's own retention policies (links in sections 2 and 3).
- **Emails you send us:** as in section 5.

Because we hold no game data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side except emails. For data held
by Google, use the controls in section 2, or write to us and we will help you reach Google.

## 9. Children

The game is made for teenagers and adults (13 and over) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not
knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads in the game are limited to content rated PG or
lower. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal data, for example by email, contact us and we will delete
it.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR, US state privacy laws, or Vietnam's personal data
protection law), you may have the right to: know what data is processed and why; access, correct or delete it;
object to or restrict its processing; withdraw consent at any time; and complain to your data protection authority.

- Most of these you can use directly: uninstall the game or clear its storage; Settings > Privacy options in the
  game; the advertising ID controls on your device.
- For anything else, email us (section 13). We will answer within the time the law that applies to you requires.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, the legal bases are: your **consent**, collected through Google's consent form, for
ads that store or read information on your device and for personalised ads; our **legitimate interest** in funding a
free game with limited or non-personalised ads, preventing fraud and keeping the game working; and the
**performance of a contract** when you buy an item.

## 11. Security

The game stores its data in the app's private storage on your device. Google's services encrypt data in transit
(TLS). We do not run servers that hold your data.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game changes the way it uses data, for example when it adds a service, and we
will change the date at the top. For important changes we will also tell players in the game's release notes on
Google Play.
